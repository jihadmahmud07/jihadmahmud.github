MY ROOTS

My Homeland

Bangladesh is my birthplace and the country that will always remain special to me. Its rivers, green nature, villages and culture are part of my identity and memories.

Even though I am living in Japan now, I always remember my family, friends and homeland.

MY BEAUTIFUL HOMETOWN

Bhola

Bhola is my hometown in southern Bangladesh. It is the country's largest island and is surrounded by the Meghna and Tetulia rivers.

I remember its green fields, coconut trees, boats, river views and peaceful village life. These places make Bhola special to me.

I hope to visit my hometown again and enjoy its beautiful nature.
